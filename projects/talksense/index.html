TalkSense

Learn anything in 20 minutes, then prove it out loud.

Open the live site ↗ View the code ↗

● liveTypeScriptgithub.com/codealisha99/talksense

What it is

Pick a topic and a study time. TalkSense gives you a brief of three to five things you should be able to explain, then times your study session.

When the timer ends, you explain the topic out loud. Your voice is transcribed and graded against a fixed four-part rubric, and you can share a public result page.

How it works

  1. Brief: 3–5 concrete objectives, key terms and places to start.
  2. Study: a dial timer for 15, 20 or 30 minutes.
  3. Explain: speech is transcribed with OpenAI using your own key, which stays in your browser.
  4. Grade: coverage 35%, accuracy 30%, depth 20%, clarity 15%. The total is computed on the server, so shared scores can't be forged.
