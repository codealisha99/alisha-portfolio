Oct 2026

Video calls where nothing touches a server

Zentro is peer-to-peer video in the browser. You share a link, your contact joins — no accounts, no downloads.

Video and audio go directly between the two people over WebRTC. The only server is a small WebSocket signaling service that pairs two clients per room and relays the offer/answer and ICE candidates. It keeps no history; once the call connects, it is out of the loop.

There is an optional transcript, generated with Whisper, for when you want notes from the call.
Oct 2026

Paper trading, real market data

OrbitLedger is a paper-trading workspace for BTC, ETH and SOL. Collateral is virtual USD; execution quotes are simulated from Binance public trades, so prices feel real while nothing real is bought or sold.

Leveraged positions are simulated linear perpetuals with a fixed, educational funding rule — 1 basis point every 8 hours. The dashboard reconciles cash, margin and P&L so you can see exactly where the numbers come from.
Nov 2026

Teaching agents to read a codebase

Sherlock started from one sentence: “give me a deep, structured, understandable mental model of an unfamiliar codebase.”

Instead of a chat bot that guesses, it runs locally as a pipeline of agents that each own a part of the job, and hands back a map you can actually reason with.

Its sibling, AgentRuntime, is about the other half: agents can only call typed, granted, sandboxed tools, and every hop is checked for PII, injection and policy.
May 2026

Big messages in a gossip network

Gossip protocols are great at spreading small messages fast. Large ones are another story — every peer forwarding a full copy wastes a lot of bandwidth.

For C4GT’s DMP 2026 I worked on Gossipsub 1.4 large-message handling: an RFC-style specification and a Python prototype of the libp2p pubsub extension.
