The case for ugly first drafts

Every good thing I’ve made started as something I’d be embarrassed to show. The problem is that tools now make it too easy to start pretty — a polished component library, a nice default font — and pretty is expensive to throw away.

So I start in grey boxes and Comic Sans on purpose. If the idea still works when it looks like that, it’s a real idea. If it only works when it’s pretty, it’s a mood board.

Then I edit. Editing is where the design actually happens.
19 Aug 2026

What a receipt taught me about hierarchy

A receipt has one font, one size, one colour, and yet you can find the total in under a second. Everything is done with position, rules, caps and whitespace.

I pinned one above my monitor as a reminder: before you reach for another font weight, try a line, a gap, or simply moving the thing.
7 May 2026

Design systems are gardens, not buildings

You don’t finish a design system. You plant some things, see what the teams actually use, pull out what doesn’t grow, and accept that someone will always plant a weird button in the corner.

The best thing I did at Fieldnote was add a “compost” folder: deprecated components go there for one release before they’re deleted. People stopped being scared of removing things.

Notes from 60 user interviews

  • People describe what they did, not what they want. Listen to the verbs.
  • The best question is “can you show me?”
  • Silence is a tool. Count to five before you fill it.
  • The workaround is the feature request.
